Global/persistent elements (appear on every page, not just Home):

- **Top utility bar** — phone, hours, client-login link (from Keating Agency)
- **Mega-menu nav** — Personal / Business / Specialty drop-downs (from Ferguson McGuire, Cusmano)
- **Sticky "Get a Quote" CTA** in the header (from Hippo's low-friction quoting)
- **Chat assistant** bubble for quick questions / quote hand-off (from Lemonade's Maya)

## Home

Address-first quote hero, KPI trust strip (carriers represented, years
operating, avg. rating, avg. claim response), coverage picture-board
grid, carrier logo carousel, and the "Independent Agency Advantage"
explainer.

## 1. Personal Insurance (mega-menu)
Auto, Home, Renters, Condo, Landlord/rental property, Umbrella, Life,
Motorcycle/boat/RV, Flood, Pet. Cross-linked via a coverage ribbon.

## 2. Business Insurance (mega-menu)
General liability, BOP, Workers' comp, Commercial auto, Professional
liability/E&O, Cyber liability, plus by-industry pages (contractors,
restaurants, retail, real estate, professional services, healthcare
practices, tech & startups, …) with an industry finder tool.

## 3. Specialty Insurance (mega-menu)
High-value home, Classic & collector car, Wedding & event, Jewelry &
valuables, Nonprofit/association, Farm & agribusiness.

## 4. Get a Quote
Start a quote (address-first, one field), compare carriers side by
side, bundle & save, request a callback.

## 5. Claims
How to file by carrier, claims support & contact, real claim-timeline
customer stories, claims FAQ.

## 6. Why Akazie
Our story, the independent agency advantage, meet the team, carriers we
represent (carousel), reviews & testimonials, careers.

## 7. Learning Center
Articles & guides, interactive coverage calculators, guides by life
stage, general FAQ.

## 8. Client Portal
Login, policy documents & ID cards, billing & payments, request a
change (endorsement), refer a friend.

## 9. Contact & Locations
Office locations & hours, phone/email by department, live chat
assistant.

## Footer
Privacy policy, terms of use, licensing & NPN disclosures, accessibility
statement, about, careers, press, contact, FAQ, file a claim, social.
