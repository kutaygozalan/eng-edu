## Positioning

"The independent agency advantage, made effortless." Akazie is an
independent agency with the reach to shop 30+ carriers, presented with
the clarity and confidence of the best insurtech products, not a
traditional agency site.

What's borrowed from the direct references, specifically:

- **Hippo** — low-friction, address-first quote start
- **Policygenius** — marketplace-style multi-carrier comparison, educational content as trust-building
- **Clearcover** — fast-claims storytelling with real customers, subtle motion
- **Oscar Health / Kin** — warm, real, non-sterile photography
- **Next Insurance** — plain language, industry/life-stage-specific pages
- **Ladder Life / Squeri** — interactive calculators and a KPI stats bar

## Voice & tone

Traits: **clear-headed, warmly direct, quietly confident, un-jargony.**

- Avoid: "Individuals seeking comprehensive protection solutions should contact an agent."
- Akazie: "Tell us your address. We'll show you real quotes from carriers that actually cover your area."

## Logo

The supplied mark: a circular canopy built from overlapping leaf-strokes
that never quite close, roots below and growth above. Supplied in gold;
that color is retired in favor of the brand's two working colorways:

- **Ink on Fog** — primary
- **Paper on Ink** — reversed, for dark sections

Minimum size ~20px (below that the interior gaps close up and it reads
as a solid blob). Clearspace on every side equals the width of one
canopy gap. Never stretch, skew, recolor outside ink/paper, or add
outlines/shadows/gradients.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |

| Harbor Ink | `#0F2E2C` | Headlines, nav, dark sections |

Dark-mode equivalents are defined in the rendered guide.

## Typography

- **Display — Fraunces**: headlines, pull quotes, italic for warmth.
- **Body — Public Sans**: all reading copy, ~65-character measure.
- **Mono — Space Mono**: nav labels, KPI numbers, data.

## Imagery & icons

Real customers and real light over stock gloss (Kin/Oscar direction).
Icons are a single 1.5px line weight on a consistent grid — no filled
umbrella/shield/handshake clip art.

## Motion

150–200ms, ease-out only, and every transition respects
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Do / don't

**Do** — one CTA color per screen; plain-language coverage terms; real
carrier names and real numbers on the KPI strip.

**Don't** — stack CTA colors; use umbrella/shield clip art; write in
passive, policy-document diction outside the actual policy documents;
publish a stat the agency can't stand behind.
